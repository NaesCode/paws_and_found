Paws & Found

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Paws and Found collects the following data to provide animal recovery and community matching services:

  • Account Details: Email, display name, and avatar URL provided via Google Sign-In or email registration.
  • Animal Reports: Photographs, physical descriptions, species classifications, and sighting timestamps.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates attached to lost or spotted animal reports to populate the community animal map.

2. How We Use Information

  • To match spotted animal sightings with lost pet alerts via computer vision embeddings.
  • To display active reports on the interactive community map.
  • To verify and secure user sessions.

3. Security & Row-Level Protection

User contact details (phone numbers and home addresses) are strictly secured using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. We do not sell or monetize personal data.

4. Contact

For questions or data deletion requests, contact our project repository at GitHub.
